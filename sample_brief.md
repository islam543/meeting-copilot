# Meeting brief: Motor claims chatbot pilot kickoff

## Purpose
Agree how we run a 3-month pilot of a chatbot that takes first notice of loss for motor claims.

## Desired outcome
Agreed pilot scope, a launch date everyone accepts, and one owner per workstream.

## My role
Product designer. I care that the pilot measures whether customers actually finish a claim in the chat.

## Attendees
- Ahmed, claims operations manager
- Sara, IT project manager
- Omar, compliance

## Background facts
- Security review for any new customer-facing channel takes 6 weeks.
- Pilot budget is approved for 3 months only.
- Phone intake takes 18 minutes on average today.

## Must cover
- Success metric for the pilot
- Who owns the budget
- Arabic language support

## Avoid
Promising a launch date before the security review is booked.
