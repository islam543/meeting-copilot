# Meeting brief: <topic>

Fill this in before each meeting. The copilot reads all of it, so short and specific beats long.

## Purpose
Why this meeting exists, in one or two lines.

## Desired outcome
What "done" looks like when you leave the room. Example: agreed pilot scope and one owner per workstream.

## My role
Who you are in this meeting and what you care about.

## Attendees
- Name, role, what they care about

## Background facts
Numbers, dates, decisions already made, constraints. The copilot uses these to catch wrong facts.

## Must cover
- Topics that must come up before the meeting ends

## Avoid
Things you should not commit to or say.
